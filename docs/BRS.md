# Business Requirements Specification – PumpSave

| ID | Requirement |
|----|-------------|
| BR-1 | Reduce the electricity cost of pumping into a tank by moving pumping to cheaper tariff periods. |
| BR-2 | Keep the tank level inside its limits when only a forecast of demand is available. |
| BR-3 | Show how the saving depends on the assumed tariff spread. |
| BR-4 | State clearly that data are benchmark-network data and that tariff and pump energy are assumptions. |

Out of scope: pump curves and efficiency, network pressure constraints, real tariffs, field pumping data.
